Mark Gabriel "Gabb" Salvacion

A narrative of passion, frequency, and precision

Known widely by his peers and audience as Gabb Salvacion, Mark Gabriel is a graduate of BS Computer Engineering from PHINMA University of Pangasinan. While his days were rooted in code logic, hardware debugging, and architectural diagrams, his evenings have always resonated with melodies, rhythms, and artistic storytelling.

Holding a guitar and penning down verses since the age of 15, Gabb emerged as a beloved local artist from Malasiqui, Pangasinan. He made waves across the regional music scene as the creative mind behind the viral trend "Raining in Dagupan"—an ode inspired by Lola Amour’s hit that struck a chord with Pangasinenses everywhere.

♫
Angat at Sikat CEASC Official Anthem · Written & Composed by Gabb Salvacion

During his collegiate journey, Gabb clinched the championship in the College of Engineering and Architecture (CEA) Songwriting Competition 2024 and earned 2nd Runner-Up at the Lamparaan Songwriting Festival 2024. Before capping off his academic career, he left an enduring legacy: composing "ANGAT AT SIKAT" alongside his bandmates and supported by the CEASC 2025—an inspiring anthem honoring the grit, camaraderie, and relentless heart of CEA students.

Beyond sound and software, Gabb possesses an eye for visual emotion. As a dedicated freelance photographer and videographer, he supported his education by immortalizing milestone moments—from weddings and grand debuts to christenings, milestone birthdays, and institutional events.
